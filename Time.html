Balancing School & Sports

Managing my time is key to being a successful student athlete. Here are some tips that help me:

  1. Use a planner to track assignments and practice times.
  2. Stay organized and don’t procrastinate.
  3. Make time for rest and recovery.
  4. Ask teachers for help when needed.
  5. Prioritize healthy habits—eat well and get enough sleep!

Here's what a typical day looks like for me:

  • 7am – Wake up & breakfast
  • 8am-3pm – School
  • 3:30pm-5:30pm – Practice
  • 6pm – Homework
  • 8pm – Family time/relax
  • 10pm – Bedtime
